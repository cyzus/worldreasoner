# 📝 Event & Graph Annotation Guideline

## 1. Annotation Objective
The goal of this annotation task is to verify the factual correctness and causal validity of the "Hindsight Graph" generated by the AI agent. Since the Hindsight Graph serves as the "Ground Truth" structure to evaluate LLM forecasting capabilities, its quality must be rigorously validated.

## 2. Scope
- **Sample Size**: Randomly sample 50 complex event forecasting questions.
- **Targets**: Discrete events identified by the Hindsight Agent and their causal impact on the forecast outcome.
- **Tools**: Use the built-in CLI command `wr evidence review -q <question_id>`.

## 3. Annotation Criteria

For each Event, you must decide whether to **Approve (A)**, **Reject (R)**, or **Skip (S)**, based on the following three dimensions:

### Criterion A: Factual Correctness
*   **Question**: Did the event actually occur, and are the dates/details accurate based on the retrieved article snippet?
*   **Approve**: The article snippet clearly supports that the event happened, and the timeline is factual.
*   **Reject (Hallucination)**: The agent fabricated details or mistook a "predictive/commentary" article for a "factual occurrence."

### Criterion B: Impact Validity
*   **Question**: Does this event have a logical and realistic causal impact on the final outcome of the forecast question?
*   **Approve**: The event directly contributed to the outcome (e.g., "Biden dropping out" directly impacts "Who will win the election").
*   **Reject (Noise / Over-interpretation)**: The event is real but has little to no causal relationship with the forecast question (e.g., pulling a news story about a minor illness of a CEO when forecasting macro interest rates).

### Criterion C: Granularity
*   **Question**: Is the event an atomic occurrence rather than a summary of the whole question?
*   **Approve**: Described as a specific action or statement (e.g., "OpenAI releases GPT-4 on March 14").
*   **Reject**: The event is too broad (e.g., "AI industry develops rapidly in 2023") and cannot serve as a clear node in a causal graph.

## 4. Edge Cases
- **Auto-approved Outcome Events**: News confirming the final outcome are automatically approved by the system. Please double-check if they are genuinely the final outcome declaration.
- **Redundant Events**: If two events describe the exact same occurrence, Approve the one with richer information/clearer timestamp, and Reject the other (Note reason: "Duplicate").
