E Independent evaluation axes

No single metric is asked to stand in for forecast quality
ACC

Outcome quality

Compares the submitted answer and probability with the objectively resolved outcome.

Measures: correctness and calibration.
Metrics: Accuracy, Brier score, log score.

SrcP

Evidence quality

Checks whether sources cited by the forecast are valid and align with the approved evidence set.

Measures: grounding and citation behavior.
Metric: Source Precision.

KER

Reasoning quality

Tests whether the agent identifies specific dated key events supported by the hindsight reference.

Measures: recovery of important event structure, not unique causal truth.
Metrics: KER and event matching.
